The Tapas Fusion

The Strategy: Cultural Hacking

We don’t ask Spanish consumers to cook Australian food; we ask them to elevate Spanish food.

By positioning Mitani not as a niche spice, but as a direct upgrade to standard finishing salt.

Visual Proof (Influencer Partnerships):

We transition from mystery to utility by showing, not telling. We partner with top Spanish food creators to replace traditional sea salt on beloved classics. This visually proves its versatility across the Spanish tapas menu.

Experiential Sampling (The Festival Circuit):

To capture the Gen Z and Millennial "Foodie Explorer" demographic, we launch bright orange Mitani food trucks at major summer music festivals (like Primavera Sound and Mad Cool). We serve one simple, free menu item: hot chips generously coated in Mitani. This links the flavor to high-energy social moments and casual dining.

The Home Kitchen Takeover (UGC):

Through the "Crea Tu Propio Challenge," we gamify the cooking experience. By incentivizing everyday consumers to post their own Mitani fusion recipes on social media, we organically transition the product from a fun cultural novelty into a permanent Spanish pantry staple.
