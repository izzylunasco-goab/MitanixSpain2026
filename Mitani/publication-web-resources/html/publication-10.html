Scalability & Conversion

The Hero Product as the Spearhead

Launching a foreign B2B manufacturing brand into a proud, saturated culinary market like Spain requires a sharp edge. Mitani’s Classic Chicken Salt acts as the ultimate "Trojan Horse." By leading with a singular, culturally disruptive hero product that naturally sparks curiosity and debate, we cut through market noise significantly faster and cheaper than attempting to launch a full, generic food service portfolio. It serves as an unforgettable introduction to the Mitani name.

B2C Hype as B2B Leverage

Spanish food manufacturers, HORECA buyers, and supermarket chains are inherently risk-averse. Instead of cold-pitching a foreign brand's supply chain capabilities, this strategy reverse-engineers the traditional sales funnel. By building rabid B2C consumer demand and social proof first, we de-risk the B2B pitch. When consumers are actively searching for "El Secreto Aussie," retail buyers and restaurant chains are incentivised to listen.

Scaling The True Revenue Engine

The B2C chicken salt buzz opens the door, but Mitani’s manufacturing capabilities build the house. Once brand trust and market viability are established via the hero product, Mitani can seamlessly pivot to its high-volume, high-margin operations. We convert the initial trend into long-term infrastructure by upselling private-label dry blending for snack brands, bulk sauces like Umami Mayo for restaurant chains, and frozen extruded tapas for mass catering.
