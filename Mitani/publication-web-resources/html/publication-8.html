The Tiered Ideal Customer Profiles (ICPs)

To scale efficiently, we segment our B2B outreach into three strategic tiers based on sales cycle length and volume potential.

Tier 1: Snack Manufacturers (Fastest Conversion)

Tier 2: Multi-Unit HORECA Chains (High Volume)

Tier 3: Supermarket Private Labels (Long-Term Anchor)

Channels & Platforms

Hyper-targeted paid campaigns aimed directly at I+D (R&D) Directors, Food Service Buyers, and Category Managers in Spain.

Bypassing crowded digital inboxes by sending the physical "Secreto Aussie" sample box directly to the corporate desks of Tier 1 and Tier 2 decision-makers.

Leveraging major Spanish food industry events like Alimentaria or Salón Gourmets.

Maximizing Decision-Maker Conversion

Lead with Data, Not Factory Specs: We do not open pitches by talking about Mitani's extrusion machinery. We open with the Spanish B2C viral metrics (TikTok views, IG Challenge participation, event buzz) to prove that consumer demand already exists and is waiting to be monetised.
