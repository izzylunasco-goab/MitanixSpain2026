The Company

Mitani is the brand behind the brand for some of Australia’s leading food manufacturing companies and a key part of their supply chain.

Products

Mitani Manufacturing is underpinned by the Mitani Development Centre, where a dedicated in-house product development, food science and safety team works on every product that passes through our facility.

The team not only ensures that all products have the required flavour and sensory profile but are also able to be scaled up to mass production, while retaining the quality and integrity of the product. This commitment to quality and food safety protects our client’s brands.
